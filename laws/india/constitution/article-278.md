## Article 278 — [Agreement with States in Part B of the First Schedule with regard to certain financial matters.]

278. [Agreement with States in Part B of the First Schedule with regard
to certain financial matters.].—Omitted by the Constitution (Seventh
Amendment) Act, 1956, s. 29 and Sch.(w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with agreements made between the Central Government and the State Governments concerning specific financial matters listed in Part B of the First Schedule. However, this specific provision has been removed from the Constitution by an amendment act.

### Key Points
* This article was about formal agreements on certain financial issues between the Centre and States.
* It dealt with specific financial matters detailed in Part B of the First Schedule.
* The provision was officially omitted (removed) from the Constitution by the Seventh Amendment Act, 1956.

### Why It Matters
Since this article is omitted, it no longer exists as a current part of the Constitution, meaning these specific agreements are governed by other existing laws and constitutional provisions.
