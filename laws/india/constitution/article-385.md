## Article 385 — [Provision as to provisional Legislatures in States in Part B of the First Schedule.]

385. [Provision as to provisional Legislatures in States in Part B of the
First Schedule.].—Omitted by the Constitution (Seventh Amendment)
Act, 1956, s. 29 and Sch. (w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

---


### Plain English Summary
This article dealt with the provision for provisional legislatures in certain states listed in Part B of the First Schedule. However, this specific provision was removed from the Constitution by an amendment in 1956.

### Key Points
* This article is now omitted (removed) from the Constitution.
* It concerned the establishment of temporary or provisional legislative bodies in specific states.
* The provision was officially omitted by the Constitution (Seventh Amendment) Act, 1956.

### Why It Matters
Since this provision has been omitted, it no longer has any current legal effect on how legislatures are established in those specified states.
