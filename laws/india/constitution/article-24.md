## Article 24 — Prohibition of employment of children in factories, etc

below the age of fourteen years shall be employed to work in any factory or
mine or engaged in any other hazardous employment.
Right to Freedom of Religion

---


### Plain English Summary
This article strictly forbids employing children below the age of fourteen years in any factory, mine, or any other job considered hazardous. It is a protective measure designed to safeguard children from dangerous work and exploitation.

### Key Points
* Children under fourteen years old are banned from working in factories, mines, or hazardous employment.
* This rule applies to all types of employment deemed hazardous.
* The primary goal is to protect the health, safety, and development of children.
* It sets a minimum age limit for employment in dangerous settings.

### Why It Matters
This article ensures that young children are protected from harmful working conditions and exploitation, ensuring they have time for education and healthy growth.
