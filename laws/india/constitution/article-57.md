## Article 57 — Eligibility for re-election

office as President shall, subject to the other provisions of this Constitution, be
eligible for re-election to that office.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article states that the President of India is eligible to be elected to the office of President again. This means the President does not have a fixed term limit for re-election, unlike some other offices.

### Key Points
* The President of India can be eligible for re-election to the office of President.
* This eligibility is subject to other rules laid down in the Constitution.
* It addresses the possibility of a President seeking reappointment.

### Why It Matters
It establishes the constitutional framework regarding whether and how the President can seek re-election, ensuring clarity on their tenure possibilities.
