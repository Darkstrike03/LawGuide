Amendment) Act, 1956, s. 29 and Sch. (w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

### Plain English Summary
Article 380 deals with the powers and functions of the President of India, specifically concerning their role in the Union government. It outlines the constitutional framework that defines how the President exercises their authority.

### Key Points
* This article establishes the legal basis for the President's role within the Indian political structure.
* It details the specific powers and duties assigned to the President by the Constitution.
* It is a foundational part of understanding the executive branch of the Indian government.

### Why It Matters
This article is crucial because it defines the scope of the President's authority, which directly impacts how laws are enacted and policies are implemented in the country.
