## Article 144 — Civil and judicial authorities to act in aid of the Supreme Court

144. Civil and judicial authorities to act in aid of the Supreme
Court.—All authorities, civil and judicial, in the territory of India shall act in
aid of the Supreme Court.
144A. [Special provisions as to disposal of questions relating to
constitutional validity of laws.].—Omitted by the Constitution (Forty-third
Amendment) Act, 1977, s. 5 (w.e.f. 13-4-1978).]

---


### Plain English Summary
This article mandates that all civil and judicial authorities across India must cooperate and assist the Supreme Court when it is performing its duties. Essentially, it ensures that the highest court has the necessary support from the rest of the legal system to function effectively.

### Key Points
* All civil and judicial authorities in India have a duty to help the Supreme Court.
* This obligation applies universally across the entire territory of India.
* It establishes a framework for cooperation between the judiciary and other state/civil bodies.

### Why It Matters
This provision ensures that the Supreme Court has the necessary support from the system to uphold the rule of law and ensure justice is delivered effectively nationwide.
