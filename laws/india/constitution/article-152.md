## Article 152 — Definition

expression “State” does not include the State of Jammu and Kashmir.
CHAPTER II.—THE EXECUTIVE
The Governor

---


### Plain English Summary
This article clarifies that when the Constitution uses the word "State," it specifically excludes the State of Jammu and Kashmir. This exclusion is a specific legal clarification regarding the definition of 'State' within the constitutional framework.

### Key Points
* The term "State" in the Constitution, for the purposes of this provision, does not include the State of Jammu and Kashmir.
* This is a definitional clause to prevent ambiguity regarding which political entities are covered by certain constitutional provisions.

### Why It Matters
It ensures clarity in legal interpretation by explicitly defining the scope of 'State' as it applies to the rest of the Constitution.
