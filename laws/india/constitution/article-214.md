### Plain English Summary
This article deals with the establishment and jurisdiction of High Courts within different states in India. It defines which states will have a High Court and outlines their authority to hear important legal cases.

### Key Points
*   The Constitution provides for the existence of High Courts for various states.
*   These courts act as the highest judicial authority within their respective states.
*   They are crucial for administering justice and interpreting state laws.

### Why It Matters
High Courts are vital because they serve as the apex court system in a state, ensuring that citizens have access to high-level judicial redressal within their region.
