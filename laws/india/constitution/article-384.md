## Article 384 — [Council of Ministers of the Governors.]

Constitution (Seventh Amendment) Act, 1956, s. 29 and Sch. (w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with the council of ministers that are appointed to assist the Governor in the state. It outlines the structure and functions of this council, ensuring that the Governor has proper administrative support in the state.

### Key Points
*   It establishes the composition and role of the Council of Ministers headed by the Governor.
*   It deals with the relationship and coordination between the Governor and the ministers within the state administration.
*   It is governed by specific provisions laid down in the Constitution (specifically referencing the Constitution (Seventh Amendment) Act, 1956).

### Why It Matters
This article is crucial because it defines the administrative structure that helps the Governor function effectively in a state, ensuring smooth governance and coordination between the state executive and the Governor.
