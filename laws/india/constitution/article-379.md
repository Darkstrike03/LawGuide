## Article 379 — [Provisions as to provisional Parliament and the Speaker and Deputy Speaker thereof.]

379. [Provisions as to provisional Parliament and the Speaker and
Deputy Speaker thereof.].—Omitted by the Constitution (Seventh Amendment)
Act, 1956, s. 29 and Sch. (w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with the rules and provisions concerning a temporary or provisional Parliament, along with the roles of its Speaker and Deputy Speaker. However, this specific article has been removed from the Constitution by an amendment in 1956.

### Key Points
* This article is omitted (removed) from the Constitution.
* It previously dealt with the structure and rules for a provisional Parliament.
* It covered the duties and functions of the Speaker and Deputy Speaker of such a body.

### Why It Matters
Since the article has been omitted, there are no current provisions under this specific heading in the Constitution regarding provisional Parliaments or their presiding officers.
