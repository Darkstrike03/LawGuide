## Article 300A — Persons not to be deprived of property save by authority of law

300A. Persons not to be deprived of property save by authority of
law.— No person shall be deprived of his property save by authority of law.]

TRADE, COMMERCE AND INTERCOURSE WITHIN THE
TERRITORY OF INDIA

---


### Plain English Summary
This article states that the government cannot take away a person's property unless it is done according to a specific law. In simple terms, your property rights are protected, and the state must follow established legal procedures before taking anything away from you.

### Key Points
*   Property can only be taken away by a valid "authority of law."
*   This ensures that property rights are not arbitrarily removed by the government.
*   The law must clearly define when and how property can be deprived.
*   It acts as a fundamental safeguard against arbitrary state action.

### Why It Matters
This article is crucial because it guarantees that citizens have a basic right to own their property, ensuring stability and predictability in economic and personal life.
