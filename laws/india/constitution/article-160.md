## Article 160 — Discharge of the functions of the Governor in certain contingencies

160. Discharge of the functions of the Governor in certain
contingencies.—The President may make such provision as he thinks fit for the
discharge of the functions of the Governor of a State in any contingency not
provided for in this Chapter.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article gives the President the power to decide how the duties of a State Governor can be carried out in special or unexpected situations that are not already mentioned in the Constitution. Essentially, it allows for flexibility in handling emergencies or unusual circumstances by delegating the Governor's powers.

### Key Points
* The President has the authority to create rules for discharging the Governor's functions.
* This power is used only in contingencies that are not specifically covered elsewhere in the Constitution.
* It provides a mechanism for handling unforeseen situations where the normal constitutional setup might be insufficient.
* It empowers the President to manage governance when standard procedures fall short.

### Why It Matters
This article ensures that the system of governance remains functional and responsive by allowing for necessary administrative adjustments during unexpected crises or unique events.
