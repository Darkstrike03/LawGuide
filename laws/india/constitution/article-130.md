## Article 130 — Seat of Supreme Court

in such other place or places, as the Chief Justice of India may, with the
approval of the President, from time to time, appoint.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article explains that the location where the Supreme Court of India will sit can be decided by the Chief Justice of India. This decision must be approved by the President of India, and this arrangement can change from time to time.

### Key Points
* The place where the Supreme Court sits is determined by the Chief Justice of India (CJI).
* The CJI's choice of location requires the approval of the President.
* This appointment process is flexible and can be changed periodically.

### Why It Matters
This article establishes the constitutional mechanism for deciding the physical location of the Supreme Court, ensuring that the seat of justice can be managed according to the needs of the country.
