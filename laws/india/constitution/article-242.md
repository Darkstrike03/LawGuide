## Article 242 — [Coorg.]

1956, s. 29 and Sch.(w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

PART IX
THE PANCHAYATS

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with the establishment and functioning of Panchayats, which are local self-governing bodies in India. It lays down the constitutional framework for how these village councils should be set up and operate at the grassroots level.

### Key Points
*   It deals with the constitutional provisions related to Panchayats.
*   It specifies the rules and structure for the establishment of these local governing bodies.
*   It ensures that local self-governance is a fundamental part of the Indian democratic structure.

### Why It Matters
This article is crucial because it empowers local communities to manage their own affairs, ensuring that governance is closer to the people who live in them.
