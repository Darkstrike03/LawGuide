## Article 10 — Continuance of the rights of citizenship

10. Continuance of the rights of citizenship.—Every person who is or
is deemed to be a citizen of India under any of the foregoing provisions of this
Part shall, subject to the provisions of any law that may be made by Parliament,
continue to be such citizen.

### Plain English Summary
This article ensures that if you are recognized as a citizen of India under the rules laid out in this part of the Constitution, you will remain a citizen. This continuity of citizenship is guaranteed, subject to any laws that the Parliament might make later on.

### Key Points
*   If a person is deemed a citizen of India under the provisions of this Part, they will continue to be a citizen.
*   This continuation of citizenship is subject to any laws that the Parliament may create.
*   It establishes a principle of stability regarding citizenship status.

### Why It Matters
This article provides legal certainty by ensuring that existing citizens do not automatically lose their status, offering stability to the rights and status of Indian citizens.
