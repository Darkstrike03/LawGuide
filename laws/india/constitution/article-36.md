## Article 36 — Definition

State” has the same meaning as in Part III.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article simply states that the meaning of the word "State" used in this Constitution is the same as it is defined in Part III of the Constitution. This ensures consistency in how the term is understood across the entire document.

### Key Points
*   The definition of "State" is found in Part III of the Constitution.
*   This article links the definition to ensure uniformity in legal understanding.
*   It confirms that the context for the term "State" is established elsewhere.

### Why It Matters
It establishes a clear and consistent foundation for understanding what a "State" is within the framework of Indian law.
