## Article 153 — Governors of States

Provided that nothing in this article shall prevent the appointment of
the same person as Governor for two or more States.]

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with who appoints the Governor for a state. It allows the same person to be appointed as the Governor for two or more different states simultaneously.

### Key Points
* Article 153 specifies the provisions related to the Governors of the States in India.
* There is an exception allowing one person to serve as Governor for multiple states.
* This article outlines the constitutional framework for the office of a State Governor.

### Why It Matters
This provision ensures administrative efficiency by allowing the central government flexibility in appointing Governors across different states when needed.
