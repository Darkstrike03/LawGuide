## Article 314 — [Provision for protection of existing officers of certain services.]

Omitted by the Constitution (Twenty-eighth Amendment) Act, 1972,
s. 3 (w.e.f. 29-8-1972).
CHAPTER II.— PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSIONS

---


### Plain English Summary
This article was about protecting the jobs and positions of certain existing officers in specific government services. It was removed from the Constitution by an amendment in 1972, meaning it is no longer a current provision.

### Key Points
* This article has been officially omitted (removed) from the Constitution.
* It dealt with providing protection for existing officers in certain government services.
* The omission occurred through the Constitution (Twenty-eighth Amendment) Act, 1972.

### Why It Matters
Since the article is omitted, it no longer has any direct legal effect on current government employment or service protections.
