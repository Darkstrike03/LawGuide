## Article 375 — Courts, authorities and officers to continue to function subject to the provisions of the Constitution

375. Courts, authorities and officers to continue to function subject
to the provisions of the Constitution.—All courts of civil, criminal and
revenue jurisdiction, all authorities and all officers, judicial, executive and
ministerial, throughout the territory of India, shall continue to exercise their
respective functions subject to the provisions of this Constitution.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article ensures that all courts, government authorities, and officials across India will keep working and performing their duties. However, they must always operate within the boundaries and rules set by the Constitution itself.

### Key Points
* All courts (civil, criminal, revenue), authorities, and officers must continue to function.
* This applies to judicial, executive, and ministerial roles throughout India.
* Their functioning is strictly limited by the provisions laid down in the Constitution.

### Why It Matters
This article establishes the fundamental framework ensuring that the entire legal and administrative machinery of the country remains operational and governed by the supreme law of the land.
