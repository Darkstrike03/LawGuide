## Article 46 — Promotion of educational and economic interests of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other weaker sections

46. Promotion of educational and economic interests of Scheduled
Castes, Scheduled Tribes and other weaker sections.—The State shall
promote with special care the educational and economic interests of the weaker
sections of the people, and, in particular, of the Scheduled Castes and the
Scheduled Tribes, and shall protect them from social injustice and all forms of
exploitation.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article requires the government to actively work to improve the education and economic opportunities for historically disadvantaged groups, specifically Scheduled Castes (SCs), Scheduled Tribes (STs), and other weaker sections of society. The state must take special care to ensure these groups are protected from unfair treatment and exploitation.

### Key Points
*   The State has a duty to promote the educational and economic interests of SCs, STs, and other weaker sections.
*   This promotion must be done with "special care."
*   The primary goal is to protect these groups from social injustice and all forms of exploitation.

### Why It Matters
This article serves as a constitutional mandate ensuring that the government takes proactive steps to bridge the socio-economic gaps faced by marginalized communities, aiming for equality and dignity.
