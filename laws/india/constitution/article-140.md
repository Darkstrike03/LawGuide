## Article 140 — Ancillary powers of the Supreme Court

law make provision for conferring upon the Supreme Court such supplemental
powers not inconsistent with any of the provisions of this Constitution as may
appear to be necessary or desirable for the purpose of enabling the Court more
effectively to exercise the jurisdiction conferred upon it by or under this
Constitution.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article allows the Parliament to give the Supreme Court extra powers or abilities that are needed to perform its duties effectively. These supplemental powers must not contradict any existing rules in the Constitution. Essentially, it ensures the Supreme Court has the necessary tools to do its job well.

### Key Points
*   The Parliament can grant the Supreme Court supplementary powers.
*   These powers must not conflict with any other part of the Constitution.
*   The powers granted are only if they are deemed necessary or desirable for the Court's effective functioning.
*   The purpose is to enable the Court to exercise its jurisdiction more effectively.

### Why It Matters
This article ensures that the Supreme Court has the flexibility and authority required to handle complex legal matters efficiently, without undermining the fundamental structure of the Constitution.
