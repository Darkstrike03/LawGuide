## Article 331 — Representation of the Anglo-Indian Community in the House of the People

the People.—Notwithstanding anything in article 81, the President may, if he is
of opinion that the Anglo-Indian community is not adequately represented in
the House of the People, nominate not more than two members of that
community to the House of the People.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article allows the President of India to nominate up to two members from the Anglo-Indian community to the Lok Sabha (House of the People) if the President believes this community is not adequately represented in Parliament. This provision is an exception to the general rules regarding representation.

### Key Points
* The President has the power to nominate members.
* The nomination is based on the President's opinion that representation is inadequate.
* A maximum of two members from the Anglo-Indian community can be nominated.
* This provision overrides the general rule mentioned in Article 81 regarding representation.

### Why It Matters
It ensures that the concerns and perspectives of the Anglo-Indian community are heard at the national level through parliamentary representation.
