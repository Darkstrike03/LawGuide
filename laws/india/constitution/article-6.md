## Article 6 — Rights of citizenship of certain persons who have migrated to India from Pakistan

6. Rights of citizenship of certain persons who have migrated to
India from Pakistan.—Notwithstanding anything in article 5, a person who
has migrated to the territory of India from the territory now included in
Pakistan shall be deemed to be a citizen of India at the commencement of this
Constitution if—
(a) he or either of his parents or any of his grand-parents was born
in India as defined in the Government of India Act, 1935 (as originally
enacted); and
(b)(i) in the case where such person has so migrated before the
nineteenth day of July, 1948, he has been ordinarily resident in the
territory of India since the date of his migration, or
(ii) in the case where such person has so migrated on or after the
nineteenth day of July, 1948, he has been registered as a citizen of India
by an officer appointed in that behalf by the Government of the
Dominion of India on an application made by him therefor to such
officer before the commencement of this Constitution in the form and
manner prescribed by that Government:
Provided that no person shall be so registered unless he has been resident
in the territory of India for at least six months immediately preceding the date
of his application.

### Plain English Summary
This article explains the conditions under which a person who moved from Pakistan to India can be considered a citizen of India at the time the Constitution came into effect. Citizenship is granted based on whether the person or their ancestors were born in India and specific residency or registration requirements are met depending on when they migrated.

### Key Points
*   A person migrating from Pakistan to India is deemed a citizen of India if they or their parents/grandparents were born in India under the rules of the Government of India Act, 1935.
*   If the migration happened before July 19, 1948, the person must have lived in India continuously since the date of migration.
*   If the migration happened on or after July 19, 1948, the person must have applied to be registered as a citizen by an authorized officer before the Constitution began.
*   A crucial condition for registration is that the person must have been living in India for at least six months right before applying.

### Why It Matters
This article establishes the initial criteria for determining who qualifies as a citizen of India based on migration from Pakistan, setting the foundational rules for citizenship status at the time of India's independence.
