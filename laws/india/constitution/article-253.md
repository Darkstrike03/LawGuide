## Article 253 — Legislation for giving effect to international agreements

Notwithstanding anything in the foregoing provisions of this Chapter,
Parliament has power to make any law for the whole or any part of the territory
of India for implementing any treaty, agreement or convention with any other
country or countries or any decision made at any international conference,
association or other body.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article gives the Parliament (the law-making body in India) the power to create laws to put international treaties, agreements, or decisions made at international meetings into effect within India. Essentially, it allows India to legally implement global commitments.

### Key Points
*   Parliament has the authority to make laws for the entire country or specific parts of it.
*   This power is used specifically to implement treaties, agreements, or decisions from other countries or international bodies.
*   This power overrides any previous rules mentioned in this part of the Constitution.
*   It ensures that India can legally follow up on its international commitments.

### Why It Matters
This article is crucial because it provides the legal mechanism for India to translate global diplomatic promises into enforceable domestic laws, ensuring international obligations are met within the country.
