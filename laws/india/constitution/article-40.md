## Article 40 — Organisation of village panchayats

organise village panchayats and endow them with such powers and authority as
may be necessary to enable them to function as units of self-government.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article mandates that the government must set up village councils (Panchayats) and give them the necessary powers and authority to act as units of self-government. This means villages get a say in managing their own local affairs.

### Key Points
*   The Constitution requires the organization of village panchayats.
*   These panchayats must be given the powers needed to function as self-governing bodies.
*   The goal is to empower local communities to manage their own development and services.

### Why It Matters
This article is fundamental because it establishes the grassroots level of democracy, ensuring that citizens in villages have a direct voice in decisions affecting their daily lives.
