## Article 382 — [Provisions as to provisional Legislatures for States in Part A of the First Schedule.]

382. [Provisions as to provisional Legislatures for States in Part A of the
First Schedule.].—Omitted by the Constitution (Seventh Amendment) Act,
1956, s. 29 and Sch. (w.e.f. 1-11-1956).

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with the provisions for provisional legislatures (temporary state assemblies) in states that fall under Part A of the First Schedule. However, this specific article has been removed from the Constitution by an amendment in 1956.

### Key Points
* This article dealt with setting up temporary legislative bodies in certain states.
* It is now omitted from the Constitution due to a constitutional amendment.
* The provisions related to this article are no longer part of the current Constitution.

### Why It Matters
Since the article has been omitted, it has no direct legal effect on the functioning of state legislatures today.
