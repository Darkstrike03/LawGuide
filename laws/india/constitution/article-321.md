## Article 321 — Power to extend functions of Public Service Commissions

Act made by Parliament or, as the case may be, the Legislature of a State may
provide for the exercise of additional functions by the Union Public Service
Commission or the State Public Service Commission as respects the services of
the Union or the State and also as respects the services of any local authority or
other body corporate constituted by law or of any public institution.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article gives the Parliament or State Legislatures the power to give the Public Service Commissions (those that check recruitment and service matters) extra duties. These extended functions can cover services of central government, state government, local bodies, or other public institutions.

### Key Points
* Parliament or State Legislatures can create laws to expand the roles of the UPSC or SPSC.
* These expanded functions can apply to services of the Union, State, local authorities, or public bodies.
* It allows for specialized oversight beyond the standard duties of the Commissions.
* The power is exercised through legislation made by the relevant legislative body.

### Why It Matters
This article ensures that the Public Service Commissions can effectively oversee and manage the personnel matters across a wider range of government and public sector entities.
