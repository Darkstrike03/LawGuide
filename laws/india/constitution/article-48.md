## Article 48 — Organisation of agriculture and animal husbandry

shall endeavour to organise agriculture and animal husbandry on modern and
scientific lines and shall, in particular, take steps for preserving and improving
the breeds, and prohibiting the slaughter, of cows and calves and other milch
and draught cattle.
48A. Protection and improvement of environment and
safeguarding of forests and wild life.—The State shall endeavour to protect
and improve the environment and to safeguard the forests and wild life of the
country.]

---


### Plain English Summary
This article directs the government to organize farming and cattle rearing in a modern, scientific way. It also mandates taking action to protect and improve animal breeds while banning the slaughter of cows, calves, and other milk and draught cattle. Furthermore, it requires states to work to protect the environment, forests, and wildlife.

### Key Points
* The government must organize agriculture and animal husbandry using modern and scientific methods.
* Steps must be taken to preserve and improve animal breeds.
* There is a prohibition on the slaughter of cows, calves, and other milk and draught cattle.
* States are tasked with protecting the environment, forests, and wildlife.

### Why It Matters
This article sets the foundational direction for how India manages its farming sector and ensures that livestock and natural resources are managed sustainably for the future.
