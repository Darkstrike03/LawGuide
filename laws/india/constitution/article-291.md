## Article 291 — [Privy purse sums of Rulers.]

(Twenty-sixth Amendment) Act, 1971, s. 2 (w.e.f. 28-12-1971).
CHAPTER II.—BORROWING

---


### Plain English Summary
This article deals with the funds or money that are kept in reserve for the rulers (the government). It essentially refers to the financial resources controlled by the state.

### Key Points
*   It relates to the "privy purse sums" of the rulers, meaning funds kept for the government's use.
*   This provision was introduced by the Twenty-sixth Amendment Act, 1971.
*   It falls under the chapter dealing with borrowing (financial matters).

### Why It Matters
It establishes the legal framework regarding the financial resources available to the government entities.
