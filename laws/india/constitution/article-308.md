## Article 308 — Interpretation

the expression “State” does not include the State of Jammu and Kashmir.

---


### Plain English Summary
This article clarifies that when the Constitution uses the word "State," it specifically excludes the State of Jammu and Kashmir from that definition. This distinction is important for understanding which geographical or political entities are covered under specific constitutional provisions.

### Key Points
* The term "State" in the Constitution does not include Jammu and Kashmir.
* This exclusion defines the scope of certain legal provisions within the Constitution.
* It ensures clarity regarding jurisdiction and applicability of constitutional rules.

### Why It Matters
This provision is crucial for determining which laws, rights, or administrative powers apply to different regions of India under the Constitution.
